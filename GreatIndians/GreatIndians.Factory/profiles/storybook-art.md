# Shared visual direction

For generated artwork, use vibrant cinematic children's nonfiction storybook art, rich watercolour-and-gouache texture,
expressive but plausible people, dimensional light and shadow, luminous skies, clear focal points and atmospheric depth.
Use royal blue, turquoise, emerald, warm gold, sunshine yellow and coral accents without imposing invented clothing or features.
Match the actual subject's researched period, setting, profession or architecture. No generic palace substitution for an actual temple.
Never invent historical scenes, labelled scientific diagrams or formulas. Any accurate diagram must be separately constructed.
Keep complete heads and important structures/objects visible, with safe space and no destructive cropping or stretching.
Generated art must contain no lettering, numbers, labels, watermarks or pseudo-script; use closed/reverse-facing manuscripts.

Real source photographs are allowed when configured. Preserve their authentic appearance and use verified source/license metadata.
Do not repaint a real photograph into this style. Authentic incidental inscriptions can remain; added overlays and watermarks cannot.
Clearly distinguish a contemporary photograph from a historical reconstruction. Review identity, passage alignment and resolution.
The Markdown narrative is authoritative and is never rewritten by this production stage.
