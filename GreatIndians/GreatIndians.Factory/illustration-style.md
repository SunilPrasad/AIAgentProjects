# Legends of Ancient India — permanent visual style

Vibrant, premium painterly historical nonfiction for ages 8–12. Cinematic, richly painted scenes,
bright natural sunlight, expressive but respectful characters. Royal blue, turquoise, emerald,
golden yellow, coral and warm terracotta. Avoid dull sepia, dark horror tones and excessive ornament.
Use this artistic language consistently across every chapter.

Identify the exact historical subject, period, region and profession from the supplied manuscript.
Use the chapter-specific character reference and the same reference image for all its scenes.
Physical appearance is a respectful interpretation, never a claim of a verified portrait.
Period-plausible clothing, headwear, buildings and objects. No modern scientific instruments,
fantasy armour, unrelated historical figures or landmarks from the wrong period.

Compose for the requested aspect ratio. Keep the whole head, face, chin, hair and headwear visible,
with 10–15% breathing room around important features. Show important hands, manuscripts and tools
completely. Medium or three-quarter portraits rather than tight close-ups. No distorted anatomy.

NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO WORDS, NO CAPTIONS, NO TITLES, NO LABELS, NO WATERMARKS, NO TYPOGRAPHY,
NO WRITING, NO NUMBERS, NO LOGOS. Blank manuscripts or visually indistinct marks are acceptable.
No generated formulas or scientific diagrams. Mathematics remains in real typeset text; exact
educational diagrams require deterministic vector artwork, never an AI approximation.

Generate artwork only, never a whole page mock-up. The separate layout engine sets the original
manuscript as selectable text. Images use contain fitting, never destructive centre cropping.
Opening portrait 3:4; later scenes 16:9, 3:2, 4:3 or 1:1. Each scene must align with a specific
supplied passage and must not invent a new historical event or an undocumented childhood scene.
