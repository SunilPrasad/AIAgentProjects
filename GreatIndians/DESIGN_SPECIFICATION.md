# LEGENDS OF ANCIENT INDIA — MASTER DESIGN SPECIFICATION

Apply these design settings consistently to every chapter. This specification supersedes the earlier PDF's measured typography and colours. The audience is ages 8–12.

## Page layout

- Paperback trim: 6 × 9 inches (432 × 648 pt).
- Page background: #FFFDF6.
- Interior: full colour.
- Reading pages: clean, uncluttered, warm ivory.
- Begin every chapter on a new page.
- Display one chapter title only.
- Do not display secondary section headings or repeated page titles.
- Use continuous paragraphs with illustrations placed at natural narrative breaks.
- Use mirrored margins and a sufficient gutter for the final book thickness.
- Keep all text and important artwork within the print-safe area.

## Typography

- Body font: Liberation Serif Regular.
- Body font size: 12.1 pt.
- Body colour: #1D2636.
- Body line spacing: target 16.5–17.5 pt.
- Main chapter title: Liberation Serif Bold, 22 pt.
- Main chapter title colour: #0C348B.
- Chapter number: Liberation Serif Bold, 10 pt.
- Chapter number colour: #B16A14.
- Page numbers: Liberation Serif Regular, 9 pt.
- Page number colour: #213A73.
- Chapter title should wrap naturally over multiple lines if required.
- Do not reduce the body font size merely to fit more text on a page.
- Use real, selectable text with embedded fonts.

## Colour palette

- Warm ivory: #FFFDF6.
- Royal blue: #0C348B.
- Dark blue-charcoal: #1D2636.
- Warm gold: #B16A14.
- Sky blue: #4DAAF2.
- Turquoise: #28B9C2.
- Emerald green: #48A65A.
- Sunshine yellow: #F8C94D.
- Coral orange: #EF7950.

## Illustration style

Vibrant cinematic Indian historical children's storybook art, digitally painted with rich watercolour-and-gouache textures, expressive characters, detailed historically inspired Indian environments, warm golden sunlight, luminous blue skies, lush green foliage, colourful fabrics, beautiful atmospheric depth, polished professional children's publishing quality.

Avoid dull sepia, monochrome, dark muddy shadows, flat cartoon graphics, photorealism and overly busy compositions.

## Illustration composition

- Generate illustrations individually, never as screenshots of book pages.
- Main character must be historically appropriate.
- Keep the entire head, hair and headwear visible.
- Leave 10–15% safe space above the head.
- Avoid tight portrait cropping.
- Never crop important faces, hands or objects.
- Use the correct aspect ratio for the final layout.
- Do not stretch or destructively crop images.
- Do not generate text, titles, captions, formulas, labels, numbers or watermarks inside artwork.
- Add any necessary educational diagrams separately using accurate vector graphics.
- Aim for 300 effective PPI at the printed dimensions.

## Manuscript

- Preserve all original researched paragraphs exactly.
- Do not paraphrase, summarise, simplify or invent material.
- Use only the original chapter title.
- Suppress secondary headings visually while retaining them in the source.
- Verify PDF and EPUB text against the source Markdown.

## Export

- PDF: print-quality 6 × 9-inch pages, correct margins, embedded fonts and selectable text.
- EPUB: reflowable HTML-based text, responsive illustrations and adjustable font sizes.
- Render and visually inspect every PDF page before delivery.
- Reject any page with cropped heads, wrong historical figures, unwanted text inside illustrations, clipped text or low-resolution artwork.
